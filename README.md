# 📌 Overview

> ApplyAI is a personal AI-powered job application system. It watches the job sources you follow, understands text and image-based listings, filters for roles that actually fit your career direction, prepares truthful tailored application materials, and asks for your approval on WhatsApp before anything is ever submitted.

> 🛠 **Status:** In active development — architecture finalized, career profile being built, pipeline coming online incrementally.

This project is designed to help you:
- ✅ Never miss a relevant job from the channels you follow
- ⚡ Skip the repetitive tailoring work behind every application
- 🎯 Only spend time on jobs genuinely worth your time
- 🛡 Never apply blindly — every submission needs your approval

---

# ✨ Features

## 🔥 Core Features

- 📥 Job ingestion from WhatsApp — text and image/screenshot listings
- 👁 Vision + LLM extraction into structured job data
- 🔍 Duplicate detection across sources and formats
- 🧑‍⚖️ Career Gatekeeper — scores every job against your profile, skills, and hard rules
- 📄 Tailored CV generated per job — never invents qualifications
- ✉️ Custom cover letters when appropriate
- ⚠️ Scam / risk detection before any application is prepared
- ✅ WhatsApp approval flow — approve or reject right from your phone
- 📊 Full application tracking and status history
- 🔁 Learns from your approval/rejection decisions over time

# 🧠 AI Features

This project integrates AI capabilities using:
- ✨ Three specialized AI roles: **Job Analyst** ("what is this job?"), **Application Builder** ("how do we present real qualifications?"), **Career Gatekeeper** ("is this worth your time?")
- 📏 Hard rules that constrain and override AI recommendations
- 🚫 A strict no-invention policy — the career profile is the source of truth
- 🔁 Personalization from your approval/rejection history over time

---

# 🔒 Security

- ✅ Human approval required before every single submission
- 🛡 Hard rules override AI recommendations
- 🚫 No invented qualifications, experience, or skills — ever
- 🔑 Secrets and credentials managed securely, never hard-coded
